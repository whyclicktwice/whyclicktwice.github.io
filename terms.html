Use and support

Effective September 20, 2026.

Scope

Jeffrey Local AI Workspace is a personal utility operated by Jeff Frederick. It is not offered as a public hosted service. This website provides app information for Google authorization and does not give visitors access to the connector or any connected account.

Authorized use

Use the connector only with accounts and documents you are authorized to access. Google consent determines available permissions; each action must also match the account owner’s instructions. The owner remains responsible for reviewing consequential changes, sharing settings and AI-generated output.

Service conditions

Google services and each configured AI application remain subject to their own applicable terms, limits and settings. Availability, quotas and authorization can change. The connector’s upstream open-source software has its own license. This information page does not offer a paid subscription, guarantee uninterrupted service or replace those terms.

Stop using the connection

Remove the connector from local AI applications and revoke its access in Google Account Connections. See the privacy notice for storage, retention and deletion details.

Support

Report setup issues through the project’s public issue tracker. Include only non-sensitive descriptions. Never include credentials, private files or authentication links.
