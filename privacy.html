Privacy notice

Effective September 20, 2026. This notice describes Jeffrey Local AI Workspace, a personal utility operated by Jeff Frederick.

Information accessed and why

With the account owner’s Google authorization, the connector accesses account identity, file metadata and requested content in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides. Depending on the owner’s request, it can search, read, create, edit, export, organize or manage access to files. These permissions support document work requested through configured local AI applications.

Where data is processed

The connector runs locally and communicates with Google APIs. Requested file content and tool results are returned to the calling AI application and may be sent to that application’s configured AI provider. Provider processing and retention depend on the selected service and account settings. Google and those providers may retain service, security and operational records under their applicable policies.

The connector does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or send it to a separate hosted connector service. Google Workspace API data must not be used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. The owner must use AI services and settings that exclude this data from such training.

Storage and retention

Google authorization credentials are stored in permission-protected files on the owner’s computer and used to maintain the authorized connection. These files are not stored on this public website. Local output files, diagnostic logs and AI conversation records may contain data from requested operations and remain until removed under their respective local or provider controls. There is no claim of automatic deletion or zero retention.

Control and removal

The account owner can revoke the utility in Google Account Connections, remove its configuration from local AI applications, and remove locally stored credentials and outputs. Revocation prevents future authorized access; it does not undo earlier document changes or delete records already retained by Google or an AI provider.

Limited use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access is limited to providing the personal document features described here.

This website

This static information site contains no sign-in, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms or advertising. GitHub Pages supplies hosting and may process ordinary web-request information under the GitHub privacy statement.

For questions or corrections, use the project’s public issue tracker. Do not post private documents, account details or credentials there.
